NONG SAN 360 - VAN HANH TIN TUC THI TRUONG V8.5
1. Trang chu chi hien THONG TIN CO NGAY CONG BO MOI NHAT (1 tin). Bấm tieu de hoac "Doc tin day du" de mo trang bai viet ngay tren website.
2. Muc Tin tuc thi truong (#/tin-tuc/index.html) liet ke tat ca theo ngay, moi nhat truoc, co loc Ca phe/Ngo/Lua mi va tim kiem.
3. Moi tin co trang chi tiet (#/tin-tuc/<id>.html), noi dung tom luoc, thong tin ngay/nguon, nut doc bao cao goc; chi nut bao cao goc moi mo website ben ngoai.
4. CAP NHAT TIN MOI: Chinh sua tin-tuc.json, them item co id duy nhat, published ISO yyyy-mm-dd, title, summary, commodity, source, sourceName, dataPeriod, url, lead, body [{heading, paragraphs:[...] }], key_takeaway, status='published'. Host website qua HTTPS; index.html tu doc tin-tuc.json va cap nhat trang chu/danh sach theo ngay, khong can sua giao dien. Tin chua duoc xac nhan: status='draft' khong hien thi.
5. Khi mo truc tiep index.html offline, giao dien dung 3 ban tin gan nhat dong goi trong HTML, vi trinh duyet chan fetch JSON bang file://. De thay tin moi ngay ca trong offline ZIP, can tai xuat HTML fallback.
6. Ban V8.4, V7 va 21 giao trinh goc duoc giu nguyen, PDF van trong thu muc pdf/.
7. Can duyet noi dung, nguon va ngay dang moi tin truoc khi chuyen status sang published. Khong co gia realtime.
